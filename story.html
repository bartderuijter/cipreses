Chapter 01

The Origin

Our home is on a hill. Not a dramatic one — just high enough that you can see the sea from the kitchen window, and feel the Tramontane when it comes down from the north. The hill is surrounded by cypress trees. Tall, narrow, ancient things that have watched this island change around them without ever changing themselves.

CIPRESES began there, not as a business plan or a market opportunity, but as a response to a problem we kept having. We would go to the beach — Ses Salines, usually, or sometimes Cala Vedella when we wanted to escape the summer crowds — and then we would want to have lunch afterwards at one of the small restaurants along the road. And we never had the right bag.

Everything beach-oriented felt wrong at a restaurant table. Everything elegant enough for lunch was wrong on the sand. The gap between those two moments was something no one seemed to have addressed, at least not in the way we cared about.

Ses Salines Beach, Ibiza

"The gap between beach and lunch was something no one had addressed — at least not in the way we cared about."

So we started designing. Not for trend or for market research, but for that specific moment — the walk from the water's edge to the restaurant, sandy feet still drying, conversation already turning toward the afternoon.

Chapter 02

The Cypress

We named the brand for the trees because the trees embody what we are trying to make. A cypress is singular. It does not spread or sprawl. It grows in one direction — upward — with a kind of conviction that reads, from a distance, as elegance.

The cypress has been here for centuries. It was planted not for decoration but because it works — as a windbreak, as a boundary marker, as shade. It is both beautiful and functional in the way that the best things always are.

The Cypress Trees, Our Hill

We think about our leather the same way. A good piece of vegetable-tanned leather does not try to be decorative. It is honest about what it is. And over time — with use, with sun, with the particular salt air of a Mediterranean coast — it becomes more itself, not less.

"The cypress grows in one direction — upward — with a kind of conviction that reads, from a distance, as elegance."

Chapter 03

The Objects

We started with two things because two was enough. A large bag and a small pouch that fits inside it and can be detached. That is the system. It sounds simple because it is simple — and simplicity, in our experience, requires more precision than complexity.

The bag is large enough to carry a day — towels, a book, a change of clothes, sunscreen, a bottle of water. But it is structured enough that it does not slump at a table or on the back of a restaurant chair. The proportions are deliberate. The hardware is solid brass, the same hardware used on luggage made to last a generation.

The Bag, Natural Leather

The pouch is the thing we use every day, even when we are not going to the beach. It holds a phone, keys, a card, cash, a lipstick. Everything you need and nothing you do not. It works as a clutch. It attaches to the bag with a magnet that clicks with the satisfying weight of good hardware.

Chapter 04

The Philosophy

We are not interested in fast fashion, or in releasing collections every season, or in chasing the particular aesthetics that tend to define "beach luxury" — the rope, the rattan, the turquoise, the bold-printed textile that is fashionable for one summer and dated the next.

CIPRESES is slower than that. We make objects that are designed to be used for a long time and to improve with that use. The leather will patina. The brass will develop a warm tone. The bag will take the shape of the things you carry in it. This is not a flaw. This is the point.

"We make objects designed to be used for a long time and to improve with that use."

We do not have a factory. We work with a small atelier — a family workshop in Portugal with three generations of leather knowledge — that makes each piece by hand, in small runs, with a lead time that reflects the care involved. There will always be a wait. We think the wait is part of the experience.

This is a small brand, and we intend to keep it that way for as long as keeping it that way serves the quality of what we make. We are not trying to reach everyone. We are trying to make the right things for the people who understand why the right things matter.
